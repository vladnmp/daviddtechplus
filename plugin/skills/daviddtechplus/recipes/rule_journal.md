# Rule journal

The member's own rules, written in plain words over brief fields, checked after each close and logged with the values used.

1. The member writes each rule in their own words; the assistant restates it over brief fields (storm level, swing odds vs usual, volatility regime, 80% range, nearest level touch chance, earnings inside the window) and confirms it with the member. No default rules or thresholds.
2. After each close: one scan of the rule symbols (1 unit), briefs only where a rule needs a field the scan lacks.
3. A log line per rule: matched or not, with the values used and the data session.
4. After the horizon: the member's own review of what followed, in their journal.

**Shows:** A dated journal of the member's rules and their checks: the member's record, not DaviddTech+'s.

**Cost:** About 1-3 units per check.

**Pairs well with:** After-close desk (`after_close_desk`), Storm check (`storm_check`)

Daily units: a brief, a scan (up to 10 symbols) or a DCA pacing call is 1 unit each; re-reading a brief already pulled today (same symbol, horizon and data) is free; the market backdrop, capabilities, recipes, the supported list and the usage check are free.
