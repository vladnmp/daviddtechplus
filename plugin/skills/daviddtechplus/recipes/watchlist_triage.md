# Watchlist triage

Sort a list of up to 10 symbols into calm, normal and stormy groups before looking closer.

1. One scan of the list (1 unit).
2. Groups by storm level (1-2, 3, 4-5), each sorted by 80% range width.
3. The scan's same-bet check: which symbols moved almost as one (or as mirror images), how many independent bets the list moved like, and how strongly it moved together lately vs usually.
4. Scheduled events inside the window (the scan's scheduled-events line) and, for single stocks, whether an estimated earnings date falls inside it (swing odds note).
5. A brief for any symbol the member wants in depth (1 unit each).

**Shows:** Three short groups with the numbers behind each placement, and one same-bet line: the pairs that moved almost as one and the number of independent bets.

**Cost:** 1 unit, plus 1 per brief.

**Pairs well with:** After-close desk (`after_close_desk`), Earnings week (`earnings_week`)

Daily units: a brief, a scan (up to 10 symbols) or a DCA pacing call is 1 unit each; re-reading a brief already pulled today (same symbol, horizon and data) is free; the market backdrop, capabilities, recipes, the supported list and the usage check are free.
