# After-close desk

A short end-of-day read of the member's own list: market mood, the list in one table, details only where the member wants them.

1. Market backdrop (free): the mood across eight reference markets, with storm levels and the next scheduled events.
2. One scan of the member's list (1 unit, up to 10 symbols): range width, volatility regime, nearest levels with touch chances, swing odds and storm level per symbol.
3. Full briefs only for the symbols the member picks, by their own criteria (1 unit each); for a stock whose odds include an estimated earnings date, its earnings-days line (the median and largest move on the last up to 8 earnings days).
4. When earlier desk numbers are in the conversation: what changed since then (storm level, range width, nearest levels, earnings inside the window).

**Shows:** One mood line, one table of the list, short notes per picked symbol, and what changed.

**Cost:** About 1-4 units; the backdrop is free.

**Pairs well with:** Storm check (`storm_check`), Rule journal (`rule_journal`)

Daily units: a brief, a scan (up to 10 symbols) or a DCA pacing call is 1 unit each; re-reading a brief already pulled today (same symbol, horizon and data) is free; the market backdrop, capabilities, recipes, the supported list and the usage check are free.
