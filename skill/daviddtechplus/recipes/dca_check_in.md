# DCA check-in

For members investing a set amount on a schedule: the pacing reading per symbol at their usual time.

1. DCA pacing for up to 10 symbols (1 unit): the current reading per symbol, its meaning and its 52-week context.
2. The member's own schedule and amount stay theirs; the reading is context for them.

**Shows:** One line per symbol with the reading and what it means.

**Cost:** 1 unit.

**Pairs well with:** Storm check (`storm_check`), After-close desk (`after_close_desk`)

Daily units: a brief, a scan (up to 10 symbols) or a DCA pacing call is 1 unit each; re-reading a brief already pulled today (same symbol, horizon and data) is free; the market backdrop, capabilities, recipes, the supported list and the usage check are free.
