# Night desk

A daily after-close review of the member's watchlist: what changed since the last briefs, with details only where something moved.

1. After each close, or as a daily scheduled task in the member's own app: the market backdrop (free), the mood and storm levels of eight reference markets.
2. One scan of the member's watchlist (1 unit, up to 10 symbols); its Since last column says per symbol what changed since the earlier brief.
3. A note of any scheduled US release inside the window (CPI, jobs report, Fed decision), from the scan's releases line.
4. Full briefs (1 unit each) only for symbols with a notable change: a close outside the earlier 80% range, a level or the invalidation crossed, or the storm level up 2 or more; each brief's What changed section lists every change.
5. A short desk note: what changed, what is unusual vs usual, and the levels worth an alert with their touch chances.
6. An offer to schedule it daily with the scheduled tasks of the member's own app.

**Shows:** What changed per symbol since the last close, what is unusual, and alert levels with their touch chances.

**Cost:** About 1-4 units a day; the backdrop is free.

**Pairs well with:** After-close desk (`after_close_desk`), Storm check (`storm_check`)

Daily units: a brief, a scan (up to 10 symbols) or a DCA pacing call is 1 unit each; re-reading a brief already pulled today (same symbol, horizon and data) is free; the market backdrop, capabilities, recipes, the supported list and the usage check are free.
