# Plan check

How the member's own trade plans on one symbol could play out by a day: the chance each opens, which exit comes first and how often each profit exit is reached.

1. The member's plans in their own words: open level (or the last close), loss exit, 1-4 profit exits, optionally the loss exit moving to the open level after a profit exit, and the day.
2. One plan check (1 unit, free when the symbol's brief was pulled today) with up to 5 plans side by side.
3. The plans next to the brief's own supports, resistances and break level, and an offer to try other levels or another day.

**Shows:** Per plan: chance it opens, profit exit 1 first vs loss exit first vs neither, each profit exit's chance with reward-to-risk, and the still-open range on the day.

**Cost:** 1 unit (free after the symbol's brief today).

**Pairs well with:** After-close desk (`after_close_desk`), Rule journal (`rule_journal`)

Daily units: a brief, a scan (up to 10 symbols) or a DCA pacing call is 1 unit each; re-reading a brief already pulled today (same symbol, horizon and data) is free; the market backdrop, capabilities, recipes, the supported list and the usage check are free.
